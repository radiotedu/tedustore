# TEDUstore design direction

Reading this as a university merchandise shop for the TED University community, in a tactile editorial retail language. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2.

- TEDU leads the identity. The official TED University logo supplies the university navy (#292e6f) and red (#e41513). Warm ivory product surfaces give fabric photography room to breathe.
- RadioTEDU appears as a single collaboration, a small project credit, Albert Sans, and its own #E31E26 in that collaboration. Its broadcast motifs do not become the store's identity.
- The proposed TEDUstore wordmark is live typography, not a replacement for the official university logo. The university logo in the footer remains genuine.
- Albert Sans keeps the navigation and shopping controls familiar to the RadioTEDU community. A restrained Georgia serif headline references collegiate lettering and balances the practical sans serif.
- The hero is a tactile product still life, followed by an actual shoppable catalogue. A contrasting RadioTEDU edit and a concise explanation of the concept create varied section rhythm.
- Product cards share a retail grid because shoppers must compare prices, category and silhouette. Editorial sections have different compositions because their purpose is storytelling.
- Motion shows hierarchy: one masked title entrance, gentle hero image travel, product zoom, and drawer transitions. It has no endless decorative loops and respects reduced motion.
- Dialogs carry shadows because they overlay the shopping surface; product cards stay flat. Two radius sizes are used for controls and drawers.
- All mock products and prices are explicitly identified as conceptual. No stock urgency, reviews, sales figures, sustainability certifications, or fulfillment claims are fabricated.
- Checkout, order receipt, discount and merchant payout are browser simulations. No card numbers, financial account data, gateway, database, analytics or email integration is used.

The project is independent and deploys only its static public folder to /tedustore. The main site and all existing systems are outside its deployment scope.
