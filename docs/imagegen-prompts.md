## Shared direction

Premium, believable university merchandise photographed as a coherent studio collection. Warm ivory `#F1EEE8` backdrop, soft directional daylight, tactile fabric and honest shadows. TEDU navy with restrained red accents. No people, fictional campus imagery, university crests, promotional badges or extra text. Square product catalogue images; a wider product still life for the hero.

## Delivery

Full-resolution PNGs are kept alongside the source. Optimized WebP files are served by the site. Image dimensions and web sizes are recorded in `docs/image-manifest.json`. Names, product copy, prices, buttons and interface text remain editable HTML/JavaScript, rather than being baked into the images.
