# University store research

Reviewed 5 October 2026. These are sources for shopping strategy, not templates or assets to copy.

| Store | Observed approach | TEDUstore decision |
|---|---|---|
| [ODTÜDEN catalogue](https://odtuden.com.tr/arama) and [T-shirt detail](https://odtuden.com.tr/urun/orta-dogu-teknik-universitesi-model-2-t-shirt) | Broad campus merchandise with category, size and price filtering and product detail pages. | Start with six legible campus essentials, category filters, sorting, size choices, product details and a size guide. |
| [The Harvard Shop](https://www.theharvardshop.com/) and [its story](https://www.theharvardshop.com/pages/about-us) | Apparel, accessories and school identity collections; its student-run story is part of the store. | Give TEDUstore a university community narrative and a small RadioTEDU collaboration. Do not import Harvard's operating history or funding claims. |
| [Stanford official store](https://shop.stanford.edu/) | Search-indexed official catalogue separates clothing, accessories and everyday wear. Direct inspection was limited by its Akamai challenge; catalogue conclusions use its official indexed content. | Keep quick category navigation and practical product comparison. No conclusions drawn about inaccessible checkout internals. |
| [Oxford Saïd clothing](https://shop.sbs.ox.ac.uk/collections/clothing) and [hoodie detail](https://shop.sbs.ox.ac.uk/products/hoodie-sweatshirt-with-oxford-said-logo) | A focused clothing range, variant selection and an add-to-cart flow; institutional branding is applied consistently across garments. | Use a small, coherent navy/cream collection, visible variant selection, cart feedback and a focused checkout. |
| [TED University identity](https://www.tedu.edu.tr/kurumsal-kimlik) | Official university logo and downloadable identity materials. The current colorful SVG uses navy #292e6f, yellow #fad500 and red #e41513. | Preserve the genuine logo, use navy and red for the store, and keep yellow within the genuine emblem. |

## Visual assets

University stores' photos and layouts were not reused. Seven original images were produced with the built-in imagegen tool: six individual product mockups and one matching editorial hero. The RadioTEDU shirt was generated with its genuine logo as a supporting print reference. The website itself uses the original RadioTEDU and TED University logo assets.

## Demo boundaries

The catalogue is fictional by explicit request and is visibly labeled as conceptual. Prices, material/design descriptions, sizes, shipping choices and discount are illustrative. Checkout is not connected to any gateway. Merchant settlement calculations are illustrative, with the assumed fee shown beside the calculation. No real order, payment, shipment or payout is created.
